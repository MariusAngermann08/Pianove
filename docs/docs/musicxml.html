Importing MusicXML

Which files Pianove reads, where to find them, and what it does with them.

.musicxml.xml.mxl

Supported files

Pianove imports MusicXML, the open exchange format nearly every notation program can write:

  • .musicxml and .xml: uncompressed MusicXML
  • .mxl: compressed MusicXML (a zip), MuseScore's default export

Where to find pieces

  • MuseScore.com: huge community library; download as MusicXML.
  • IMSLP: public-domain scores, some with MusicXML files.
  • MuseScore (the free program): write or open anything and export it as MusicXML.

Importing

  1. Open Songs and press +.
  2. Choose the file. Pianove copies it to ~/.pianove/songs/, so the original can move or be deleted.
  3. The song appears in the list with its title, composer, number of bars and difficulty.

Title and composer are taken from the file's metadata, or from the title on the first page when that is all there is (as in many MuseScore exports).

What is read

Read and usedSimplified or skipped
Notes, rests, note values, dots, tripletsGrace notes are left out
Chords, ties, both staves of a piano partOnly the first part of the score is used
Key and time signatures, tempo, dynamicsSeveral voices on one staff are merged
Title and composerLyrics, repeats and slurs are not shown

The right hand is read from the treble staff and the left from the bass staff, so a normal piano score works as it is.
