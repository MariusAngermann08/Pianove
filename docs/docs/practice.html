Setting up a session

The Practice tab generates a new piece of music every time, so you read rather than remember. Choose:

  • Clef: treble (right hand) or bass (left hand)
  • Difficulty: beginner, intermediate or advanced: range, steps or leaps, note values
  • Key: C, G, D or F major
  • Notes (8 to 64) and BPM (40 to 200): length and tempo

Playing

Press Start Session. After a count-in the music scrolls in tempo; there is no waiting, as in a real sight-reading test. At the end you see how many notes you hit, and the result counts towards your practice statistics and the Sight Reader and Sharp Eye achievements.
