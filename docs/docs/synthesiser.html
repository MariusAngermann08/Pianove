Your App

Piano Sound

A sampled grand piano built in, for keyboards without speakers and for every demo.

FluidSynthSalamander Grand (C5 Light)

What it does

Pianove plays piano sound in two situations:

  • Demos: theory scenes, Listen and the editors' preview play phrases for you.
  • Your keys: with Settings → MIDI Sound Output on, every key you press on a MIDI keyboard sounds, including the sustain pedal. Leave it off if your digital piano has its own speakers.

Engine and sound

The sound engine is FluidSynth playing the bundled Salamander Grand Piano (C5 Light) SoundFont, sampled from a Yamaha C5 grand by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0). It covers all 88 keys with several velocity layers, so soft and loud playing sound different. The file is pianove/assets/soundfonts/SalamanderC5-Light.sf2; nothing has to be downloaded.

The audio buffer is kept small (256 samples at 44.1 kHz, about 6 ms) so a key sounds the moment you press it.

Volume

Settings → Volume sets the level; it is remembered between sessions.

With the microphone

When Pianove listens through the microphone it knows which notes it is playing itself, so its own sound is not mistaken for yours.
